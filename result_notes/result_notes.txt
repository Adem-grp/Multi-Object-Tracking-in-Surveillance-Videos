First 40 iterations of hyperparameter tuning for Yolov11n resulted in having the same results for 40 iterations despite
the changes in the hyperparamters. Early stopping was implemented. Therefore, all trials ended at epoch 13 and the best
epoch was always the first one.
Then to check if there was a problem with convergence early stopping was lifted and 3 trials run with 50 epochs
As a result, no important improvements were detected.

YOLO11m was decided to be used since it is more powerful and also were able to run more epochs with early stopping.
Next Experiments will be done with YOLO11m. I will check conf iou and max_det variables to observe their
affect on the result metrics and identify the best values for them.



A low confidence threshold was selected to maximise recall at the detector stage,
 as downstream tracking algorithms can suppress false positives but cannot recover missed detections.
 Empirical grid‑search evaluation showed that YOLOv11m achieves its highest recall–precision trade‑off
 at lower confidence thresholds on the GMOT dataset.
